Staff handbook, section 4: Leave

4.1 Annual leave. Every full-time employee has 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees have the same entitlement in proportion to the hours they work. You may carry over up to five unused days into the first three months of the next year.

4.2 Booking leave. Book leave in the HR portal and wait for your team lead to approve it. For one to four days, give at least one week of notice. For five or more working days in a row, give at least four weeks of notice.

4.3 Sick leave. If you are ill, tell your team lead before your shift starts on the first day. From the fourth day in a row, send a note from a doctor.

4.4 Public holidays. Public holidays are paid and are not taken from annual leave. If you work on a public holiday, you get a day off in lieu within the next two months.
