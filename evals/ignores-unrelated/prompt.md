Summarize this PDF in five bullet points. Here is its text:

Urban heat islands raise night-time temperatures in dense city districts. This study compares surface temperatures in 40 neighbourhoods of one mid-sized city over two summers, using satellite readings and 120 street-level sensors. Districts with more tree canopy were on average 2.1 degrees cooler at night than districts with little canopy, and green roofs lowered roof surface temperatures but had a small effect at street level. Light-coloured pavements reduced daytime surface heat but raised the heat felt by pedestrians around midday. The authors conclude that tree planting gives the largest night-time benefit per unit of cost, and they recommend combining it with shading at bus stops and school entrances. Limits of the study include the short period and the single climate zone.
